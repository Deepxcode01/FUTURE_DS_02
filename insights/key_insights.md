# 💡 Key Insights — Customer Churn Analysis

## 📊 Overview
- **Dataset:** 1,500 telecom customers
- **Overall Churn Rate:** ~31.8%
- **Revenue at Risk:** Significant portion of total revenue

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Contract Type is the #1 Churn Driver
- Month-to-Month customers churn at **~45%**
- One Year contracts: **~20%**
- Two Year contracts: **~8%**
- → Customers on flexible plans have 5x higher churn risk

### 2. Early Tenure = High Risk
- Customers in first **0–6 months** churn at **~50%**
- After **36+ months**, churn drops to **~12%**
- → First 6 months is the critical retention window

### 3. Fiber Optic Users Are Dissatisfied
- Fiber Optic churn: **~40%** vs DSL: **~25%**
- Despite being a premium service, satisfaction is low
- → Service quality or pricing perception issue

### 4. Payment Method Signals Commitment
- Electronic Check users churn at **~40%**
- Credit Card / Bank Transfer users churn at **~22%**
- → Auto-pay users are more committed customers

### 5. Multi-Product Customers Stay Longer
- 1-product customers: highest churn
- 3-4 product customers: significantly lower churn
- → Product bundling is a strong retention tool

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

| # | Action | Expected Impact |
|---|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Offer 20% discount to convert Month-to-Month → Annual | Reduce churn by ~15% |
| 2 | Onboarding program for first 90 days | Reduce early churn by ~20% |
| 3 | Fiber Optic quality audit + service improvement | Reduce fiber churn by ~10% |
| 4 | Incentivize auto-pay setup (cashback/discount) | Reduce payment-related churn by ~8% |
| 5 | Bundle offers for single-product customers | Increase multi-product adoption |
| 6 | Loyalty program for 24-month milestone | Improve long-term retention |
